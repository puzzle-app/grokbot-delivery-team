# Deliverables

[Back to context](README.md)

What each deliverable looks like. The skills carry the full procedure; this is the shape, so a Bot and a consultant mean the same thing by the same name.

## Updates & Deliverables deck (Delivery Lead, once per account, updated weekly)
Cover. Overview. The pain points and goals (before and after). The scope: project contacts, the engagements bought, the teams and workflows. How we work together: roles, communication stack, meeting types, what we ask of project contacts and the affected team. Phases and timeline with the request window marked. Phase details with deliverables. The weekly update slide per team. The Puzzle map and how to accept the invitation.

## Current state survey (Delivery Lead sends it with the kickoff follow-up)
Per affected team member: role, the workflows they work in, the steps they take, the tools they use, how long each takes and how often, where it hurts, what they would change.

## Interview guide (Current State Mapper, per project contact)
Built by the `interview-for-a-process` skill. The trigger, the steps in order, the handoffs, the tools and the data entered at each, the time and frequency, the exceptions, the pain, the wish.

## Recap email (Recap Writer, after every call)
Built by the `recap-email` skill. Opener. Bold topic headings in the order they came up, flat bullets, action verbs, six to eight words each. Decisions, considerations, build requests, pending items, homework with owners, useful links. Closer with the support address. One template per call type.

## Current state map (Current State Mapper, per workflow)
Built by the `build-puzzle-process` skill on the Current State tab. One section, every step complete, roles and tools on their canvases, costing inputs on every Person step, a shared view per team.

## Tech stack recommendation (Tech Stack Researcher, per system)
Two or three options from the consultant's platforms, each checked against the pain points, data, integrations and client size; priced from the vendor's page with the date; trade-offs named; a cost comparison sheet; a vet-and-demo prep.

## Future state recommendation (Process Architect, per workflow)
The change, the rationale, the trade-off, the build it needs, the homework it needs. Alongside the future state map on the Future State tab with pain points annotated at the steps that resolve them, and a prototype spec.

## AI Opportunity Roadmap (Process Architect, per account)
Built by the `find-automation-opportunities` skill. Every automation and AI opportunity, ranked, with the steps it touches, the prerequisites, the phase it lands in, and the figure only where duration and frequency make it defensible.

## Quantification of Impact (Impact Analyst, per account, at gate 1)
Built by the `cost-a-process` skill from Puzzle's costing and scenarios. Per workflow: current state cost, future state cost, the net in labor and tools, hours returned per month, payback. Generated from the workspace, never maintained separately.

## Build spec (Build Specialist, per workflow)
System, objects and fields, automations and triggers, integrations, test cases, client prerequisites. The build request tracker alongside it.

## Training deck (Trainer, per team)
Built by the `training-deck` skill. Cover. The improvements. The high-level info with the launch day. Training resources: the recording, the Looms, the Puzzle map, the office hours dates. One Loom slide per workflow. Step-by-step slides per workflow.

## Innovation Report (Impact Analyst, per account, after the demo)
Built by the `innovation-report` skill. The method in five steps. The metrics with their definitions and before values. The outcomes: before and after. The breakdown by department or by tool. The per-workflow table. The pain points resolved. The caveat on time saved.

## Final before-and-after recap (Trainer, at close)
The recap email that closes the engagement, worded to show the impact, with the Innovation Report attached and every link the client will need.
