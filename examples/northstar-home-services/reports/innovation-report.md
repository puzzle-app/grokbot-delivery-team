# Innovation Report: Northstar Home Services (synthetic)

2026-02-23 to 2026-04-27. 4 workflows across 2 teams. From the Puzzle workspace on 2026-04-15. Produced by the Impact Analyst with the `innovation-report` skill after the Future State Demo.

## Why and how
Northstar's jobs lived in three places that didn't talk: a spreadsheet, a whiteboard and an invoicing system, with the owner as the only person who knew the status of every job. Changes made in the office didn't reach technicians on the road, and invoices waited days for photos stuck in text threads.

Over eight weeks we identified the four workflows and the people who run them, mapped the current state step by step in Puzzle, identified where automation and visibility would remove the retyping and the texting, prototyped the future state in Workiz with each team, and built it after the owner signed off.

## Outcomes
Nine steps that used to be typed or texted by a person now happen on their own. All four handoffs between the office and the field are automated: assigning a job notifies the technician, closing a job drafts the invoice, booking a callback tells the dispatcher, and a status tap updates the office board. The whiteboard is gone. The average number of tools a person touches per workflow fell from 3.5 to 2.25, and nine of the eleven pain points from discovery are resolved with a completed changelog entry behind each.

## Pain points
Resolved: every job typed three times → entry 14, "Job intake form creates customer, job and slot". Whiteboard changes don't reach techs → entry 15, "Schedule board with push notifications". Invoices late because photos sit in texts → entry 16, "Close job in app drafts the invoice". Callback history by scrolling → entry 17, "Job history on the customer record". Five more, see the changelog.

Open: "Argue about whether a callback is covered" (no warranty terms recorded; a decision for Dana, entry 21, idea). "Chase a missing photo" (the app requires a photo to close; measure after launch, entry 22, planned).
