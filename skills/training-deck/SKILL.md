# Training deck

One deck per team, from the consultant's template, with every step taught read from the future state map. The human presents and records. The deck and the Looms are what the team keeps.

Read `context/consultant.md` (the deck template link) and the client's future state tab first.

## Inputs
- The consultant's training deck template (Google Slides or whatever `consultant.md` names).
- The future state map for the team's workflows: every step with executor, role, tool and note.
- The improvements and the pain points resolved, from the changelog.
- The launch day, the office hours dates, the recording plan, from the engagement file.
- The Loom links, once recorded.

## Slide order
1. Cover: client and project name.
2. Our work: the pain points and goals the engagement set out to solve, before and after. Carried over from the Updates & Deliverables deck.
3. Improvements: three to five, each one line, each pointing at a changelog entry.
4. High-level info: what changes on launch day, what does not, who to ask. Launch day stated.
5. Training resources: this recording, the Loom videos, the Puzzle map link, the office hours dates and times.
6. The Puzzle map: a screenshot of the team's future state section and the shared view link.
7. One Loom slide per workflow: title, the Loom embed or link, one line on what it covers.
8. Step-by-step slides per workflow: one slide per step or per group of three short steps. Title "Step n: <verb-first name>". Body: who, in which tool, what to click, what to enter, what happens next, with a screenshot placeholder.
9. Close: office hours, how to ask for help, the support address.

## Procedure
1. Clone the template; never edit it.
2. Read the future state section for the team. Every step on a step-by-step slide exists in the map, in map order.
3. Fill slides 2 to 5 from the changelog and the engagement file.
4. Write the step-by-step slides from the step notes. Where a note is missing, write the slide from the build spec and flag it.
5. Mark every screenshot placeholder with the step id so the consultant can capture it.
6. Propose the outline in chat; build after the consultant's OK.

## Rules
- Every step taught exists in the future state map. No invented steps, no invented features.
- Roles, not names, on the slides.
- The deck is a draft in the client's Drive folder until the consultant approves it.
